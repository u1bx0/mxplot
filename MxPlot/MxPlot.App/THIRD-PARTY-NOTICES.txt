Notes and distribution guidance
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The list in sections 1-3 reflects the packages detected by
  `dotnet-project-licenses`; sections 4-8 were added by hand after
  inspecting this application's actual publish output (every .dll sitting
  next to MxPlot.App's own assembly), since some of them (SkiaSharp,
  HarfBuzzSharp, Tmds.DBus.Protocol) are pulled in by Avalonia itself and
  are easy for a plain package-reference scan to miss or under-report.
- If you add further packages later, re-run the same check against the
  actual publish output (not just this project's own direct package
  references) before assuming the list above is still complete.
- Before publishing a build, verify the actual files included in the
  distribution and ensure all required license texts are present.
- Section 9 covers data vendored into MxPlot's own source rather than NuGet
  dependencies, so license scanners such as `dotnet-project-licenses` will
  not report it.
